
# Supported Mobile Devices – GSMPHIL

## Overview

GSMPHIL provides online mobile device checking services through its Free IMEI Checker.

Official website:
https://www.gsmphil.com/free/

## Device Manufacturers

GSMPHIL's mobile service platform covers various popular smartphone manufacturers.

Examples include:

- Apple
- Samsung
- Google
- Xiaomi
- Huawei
- OPPO
- Vivo
- Motorola
- Realme
- OnePlus

## Apple Devices

Apple devices may include:

## Service Availability

Supported manufacturers do not necessarily mean every device or checking service is available.

Actual availability depends on:

- Device model
- Selected checking service
- IMEI format
- Available database information
- Current service availability

## How to Check Compatibility

1. Visit https://www.gsmphil.com/free/
2. Review the available checking services.
3. Select the appropriate service.
4. Follow the website's instructions.
