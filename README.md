## What Is an IMEI?

IMEI stands for International Mobile Equipment Identity.

It is a unique identifier assigned to mobile devices, generally consisting of 15 digits.

IMEI checking can help users retrieve available device-related information through supported databases and checking services.

## How to Check Your IMEI

1. Open your phone's dialer.
2. Dial `*#06#`.
3. Copy your device's IMEI number.
4. Visit the official GSMPHIL Free IMEI Checker.
5. Enter the requested information.
6. Follow the instructions displayed on the website.

**Start checking:**

https://www.gsmphil.com/free/
