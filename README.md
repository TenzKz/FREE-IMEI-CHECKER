## What Is an IMEI Checker?

An IMEI checker is an online tool used to look up information associated with a mobile device's International Mobile Equipment Identity (IMEI).

IMEI information can help users identify and assess their mobile devices before purchasing, selling, troubleshooting, or requesting mobile services.

## How to Check an IMEI

1. Open your phone's dialer.
2. Dial `*#06#` to display your IMEI.
3. Visit the official GSMPHIL Free IMEI Checker.
4. Enter your device's IMEI number.
5. Follow the instructions displayed on the website.
