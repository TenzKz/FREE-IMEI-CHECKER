## What Is an IMEI?

IMEI stands for International Mobile Equipment Identity. It is a unique identification number assigned to mobile devices.

Most mobile phones have a 15-digit IMEI.

## How to Find Your IMEI

### Method 1: Dial Code

1. Open your phone application.
2. Dial `*#06#`.
3. Your device will display its IMEI number.

### Method 2: Device Settings

**iPhone:**
1. Open Settings.
2. Select General.
3. Select About.
4. Locate the IMEI field.

**Android:**
1. Open Settings.
2. Navigate to About Phone.
3. Find the IMEI information.

Menu names may vary depending on the device manufacturer.
